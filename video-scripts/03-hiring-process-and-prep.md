# Video 3: "The Hiring Process, Step by Step"

**Target length:** ~90 seconds (~190 words)
**Tone:** clear roadmap, demystifying — reduce anxiety by naming what's coming
**Covers:** resume stage, job/company research, interview prep, framed as one connected path

---

**[VISUAL: A simple 4-step path/trail (ties to "Path" branding) with icons: Document, Phone, People, Handshake.]**

Getting hired isn't one big test — it's four smaller ones, back to back. Knowing what's coming makes each one less scary.

**[VISUAL: Step 1 — Document icon lights up.]**

**Step one: your resume gets you in the door.** Its only job is to prove you're worth a conversation — clear, honest, and matched to the role.

**[VISUAL: Step 2 — magnifying glass over a company logo/building.]**

**Step two: research, before you even get a call.** Look up the company, the team, and what the role actually involves day to day. It shows in every answer you give.

**[VISUAL: Step 3 — Phone icon, then two people talking.]**

**Step three: the conversations** — often a recruiter first, then the hiring team. They're checking fit both ways. Bring real examples of what you've done, not just what you'd do.

**[VISUAL: Step 4 — Handshake icon, confetti.]**

**Step four: the offer** — the point of everything before it.

**[VISUAL: Trail loops back to the Document icon.]**

Notice it starts and ends with the same honesty: your resume, and how you talk about it, should be telling the same true story.

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: One path, four steps. You've got this.]**
