# Video 1: "What Is a Resume, Really?"

**Target length:** ~70 seconds (~150 words at a relaxed pace)
**Tone:** warm, direct, encouraging — "yes, you can" voice, not corporate

---

**[VISUAL: A person's day — going to work, volunteering, helping a neighbor, finishing a course. Simple, everyday moments.]**

Every day, you do things that prove you're capable. You show up, you solve problems, you help people, you learn.

**[VISUAL: Those same moments condense into short lines appearing on a single page.]**

A resume is just a one-page record of that — proof, written down, so someone who's never met you can see what you're capable of before they ever shake your hand.

**[VISUAL: Page splits into labeled sections — Experience, Skills, Education.]**

It's not your whole life story. It's the highlight reel: what you did, where, and what came of it.

**[VISUAL: Person looks nervously at a blank page, then relaxes as it fills in.]**

Here's the part people get wrong: a resume isn't about sounding impressive. It's about being *specific* — about work you've actually done.

**[VISUAL: WrenPath logo, wren perched on the branch.]**

That's what WrenPath helps with — not writing something that sounds good, but helping you recognize what's already true, and put it on the page.

**[TEXT ON SCREEN: Your resume is proof. Let's find it together.]**
